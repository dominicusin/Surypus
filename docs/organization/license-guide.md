# Choosing an Open Source License

This guide helps contributors understand the license for Surypus.

## License for Surypus

**MPL-2.0 (Mozilla Public License 2.0)**

A copy of the license is in the [LICENSE](../LICENSE) file.

## Why MPL-2.0?

MPL-2.0 is a balanced license that:

- **File-level copyleft** - Changes to MPL-licensed files must be shared
- **Compatible with proprietary code** - Can be used in proprietary projects
- **Patent protection** - Explicit patent grant from contributors
- **Simpler than GPL** - Less restrictive for commercial use
- **Interoperable** - Compatible with a wide range of licenses

Compared to other popular licenses:

| License | Copyleft | Commercial Use | Patent Grant | Complexity |
|---------|----------|----------------|--------------|------------|
| MIT | No | ✅ | ❌ | Low |
| Apache 2.0 | No | ✅ | ✅ | Medium |
| GPL v3 | Strong | ✅ | ✅ | High |
| MPL 2.0 | Weak | ✅ | ✅ | Medium |

## License Compliance

### For Contributors

By contributing to Surypus, you agree that your contributions will be licensed under MPL-2.0.

### For Users

You can:
- Use Surypus in your projects (commercial or non-commercial)
- Modify Surypus source code
- Distribute Surypus

You must:
- Share modifications to MPL-licensed files
- Retain copyright notices
