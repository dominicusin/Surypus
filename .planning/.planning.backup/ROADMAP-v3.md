# Roadmap v3.0 — AI & Advanced Features

## Phase 22: AI Integration

**Goal:** Implement AI-powered business intelligence and automation.

**Requirements:** AI-01, AI-02, AI-03, AI-04

**Success Criteria:**
- LLM integration for document processing
- Predictive analytics for sales forecasting
- Smart recommendations for inventory
- Automated data entry from documents

**Plans:** 2 plans in 2 waves

| Plan | Wave | Objective | Files |
|------|------|-----------|-------|
| 22-01 | 1 | AI infrastructure + document parsing | src/AI/*.hs, AI.hs |
| 22-02 | 2 | Predictive models + smart recommendations | src/AI/Models.hs |
