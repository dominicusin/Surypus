## Our Commitment

We are committed to:

- **Taking every report seriously** - No report will be dismissed without review
- **Responding promptly** - We acknowledge reports within 48 hours
- **Maintaining confidentiality** - Reports are handled with strict confidentiality
- **Being fair and transparent** - Our process is consistent and documented
- **Following our own rules** - Maintainers are subject to the same standards

## Reporting Process

### How to Report

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior:

1. **Email** (preferred): [INSERT_EMAIL]
2. **GitHub Security Advisories**: [Create advisory](https://github.com/surypus/surypus/security/advisories/new)

### What to Include

- Your contact information
- Names of individuals involved
- Description of the incident
- Supporting evidence (screenshots, links)
- Any additional context

### Confidentiality

All reports are handled with strict confidentiality. We will not share your identity without your consent, except when required by law.

### No Retaliation

Retaliation against anyone who reports a violation in good faith is strictly prohibited and will be treated as a Code of Conduct violation.

## Investigation Process

### Step 1: Acknowledge (within 48 hours)

When a report is received, the reporter receives:
- Acknowledgment of receipt
- Expected timeline for investigation
- Contact person for questions

### Step 2: Investigate

The investigation includes:
- Reviewing the reported behavior
- Gathering information from all parties
- Reviewing past interactions and context
- Consulting with other maintainers if needed

### Step 3: Determine

Based on the investigation, we determine:
- Whether a violation occurred
- The severity of the violation
- Appropriate enforcement action

### Step 4: Action

We take action based on the [Enforcement Guidelines](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md#enforcement-guidelines):
1. Correction (private warning)
2. Warning (with consequences)
3. Temporary ban
4. Permanent ban

### Step 5: Communicate

We communicate the outcome to:
- The reporter (with details of resolution)
- The accused (with explanation and consequences)

### Step 6: Document

All incidents are documented for:
- Consistency in future cases
- Pattern recognition
- Transparency (anonymized)

## Enforcement Actions

### Choosing the Right Action

When determining the appropriate action, we consider:

- **Severity** - How harmful was the behavior?
- **Impact** - How did it affect the community?
- **Intent** - Was it intentional or accidental?
- **History** - Is this a pattern of behavior?
- **Response** - How did the person respond to the report?

### Action Descriptions

#### Correction (Private Warning)

**When**: First-time minor violations, unintentional harm

**Action**: Private written warning explaining:
- What behavior was unacceptable
- Why it was harmful
- Expected behavior going forward

**Duration**: No ongoing restrictions

#### Warning (With Consequences)

**When**: Repeated minor violations, single moderate violation

**Action**: Formal warning with:
- Specified period of restricted interaction
- Clear expectations for behavior
- Consequences for further violations

**Duration**: 1-30 days depending on severity

#### Temporary Ban

**When**: Serious violations, sustained inappropriate behavior

**Action**: Temporary ban from:
- GitHub issues, PRs, and discussions
- Community channels
- Project events

**Duration**: 30-90 days depending on severity

#### Permanent Ban

**When**: Pattern of violations, harassment, aggression

**Action**: Permanent removal from the community

**Duration**: Permanent

## Appeals Process

### Right to Appeal

Anyone subject to enforcement action has the right to appeal.

### How to Appeal

1. **Submit appeal** within 14 days of the decision
2. **Email** [INSERT_EMAIL] with:
   - Reference to the original incident
   - Reason for appeal
   - Any new evidence or context

### Appeal Review

- Appeals are reviewed by a maintainer not involved in the original decision
- The appeal reviewer may:
  - Uphold the original decision
  - Modify the enforcement action
  - Reverse the decision
- The appeal decision is final

### Timeline

- Appeal acknowledged: within 48 hours
- Appeal reviewed: within 7 days
- Decision communicated: within 14 days

## Conflict of Interest

### When a Maintainer is Involved

If a report involves a maintainer:
- That maintainer recuses themselves from the investigation
- Another maintainer handles the case
- If all maintainers are involved, an external party is consulted

### When the Reporter is Involved

If the reporter is a maintainer:
- They may be asked to step back from the investigation
- Another maintainer takes the lead

## Documentation and Transparency

### What We Document

- Date and nature of the report
- Investigation findings
- Enforcement action taken
- Outcome and follow-up

### What We Don't Share

- Identity of the reporter (without consent)
- Private details of the incident
- Internal deliberations

### Annual Transparency Report

We publish an annual transparency report including:
- Number of reports received
- Types of violations
- Actions taken
- Community health metrics

All data is anonymized to protect privacy.

## Maintainer Responsibilities

### All Maintainers Must

- Read and understand the Code of Conduct
- Complete enforcement training (this document)
- Model the behavior we expect
- Take reports seriously
- Maintain confidentiality
- Follow the enforcement process

### Lead Maintainer Responsibilities

- Oversee the enforcement process
- Handle sensitive cases
- Update the Code of Conduct as needed
- Publish the annual transparency report
